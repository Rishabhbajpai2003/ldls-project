Harbour bridge to close for repairs

By Petra Lund · 9 October 2026

The Harbour Bridge will be closed to all traffic from 3 November to 21 November while engineers replace the bearings and resurface the deck.

The town council said the repairs will cost 4.2 million crowns, paid from the regional infrastructure fund.

Drivers will be diverted along Mill Road, which adds about ten minutes to the trip. A free shuttle boat will run between the two quays every 20 minutes from 06:00 to 23:00.

"The bearings are original from 1964 and have reached the end of their life," said chief engineer Arvo Saar.
