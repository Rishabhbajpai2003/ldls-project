Smoked Mackerel Chowder

Posted on 11 February 2026 by Maren Holt

This chowder is what I cooked on the night ferry when the weather turned. It is thick, smoky and very forgiving.

Hot-smoked mackerel works best because it flakes easily. Add it at the very end so it stays in chunks.

Recipe: Smoked Mackerel Chowder

Prep time: 20 minutes · Cook time: 35 minutes · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g hot-smoked mackerel, skin removed
  • 500 g waxy potatoes, diced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 700 ml fish stock
  • 200 ml double cream
  • A small bunch of dill

Method

  1. Soften the leek in butter for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cream, then the flaked mackerel, and heat for 5 minutes.
  4. Finish with chopped dill and black pepper.
