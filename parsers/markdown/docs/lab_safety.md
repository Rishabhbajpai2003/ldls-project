# Teaching Lab Safety Manual

This manual applies to Rooms B210 and B212 of the Halvorsen Chemistry Building. Every student must read it and sign the declaration before their first practical session.

## Personal protective equipment

- Safety goggles must be worn at all times while anyone in the room is working with chemicals.
- Lab coats must be knee-length and fully buttoned.
- Nitrile gloves are standard. Use the thicker butyl gloves (green box) when handling concentrated acids.
- Closed shoes are required; sandals are never allowed.

## Chemical storage

### Flammables

Flammable solvents are kept in the yellow cabinet next to the fume hoods. No more than 2 litres of any flammable solvent may be on a bench at one time.

### Acids and bases

Acids are stored in the blue cabinet and bases in the white cabinet. Never store them together. Nitric acid has its own shelf because it reacts with organic material.

### Labelling

Every container must show its contents, concentration, the date and your initials. Unlabelled containers are disposed of by staff at the end of each day.

## Emergency procedures

### Fire

Shout "fire", pull the alarm at the door and leave by the nearest exit. The assembly point is the car park beside the library. Use the CO2 extinguisher only on small fires and only if you have been trained.

### Chemical splash

Use the eyewash station for at least 15 minutes for splashes to the eyes. For skin contact, use the safety shower and remove contaminated clothing. Report every splash to the lab supervisor.

### Spills

Small spills under 100 millilitres may be cleaned with the spill kit under the sink. For larger spills, leave the room and call the building safety officer on extension 4471.

## Waste disposal

| Waste type             | Container                  |
|------------------------|----------------------------|
| Halogenated solvents   | Red-labelled bottle        |
| Non-halogenated solvents | Blue-labelled bottle     |
| Broken glass           | Cardboard glass box        |
| Heavy metal solutions  | Separate sealed jar, ask staff |

Never pour solvents down the sink.

## Working hours

Students may only work when a supervisor is present. Supervised hours are 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. Working alone or at weekends is not permitted.
