# Dark Rye Sourdough

A dense, tangy rye loaf baked in a tin. The recipe makes one loaf of about 1.1 kilograms and takes two days from start to finish.

## Ingredients

### Levain

- 40 g active rye starter
- 160 g whole rye flour
- 160 g water at 30 °C

### Final dough

- All of the levain
- 340 g whole rye flour
- 120 g bread flour
- 330 g warm water
- 12 g salt
- 25 g dark molasses
- 1 tablespoon caraway seeds (optional)

## Equipment

You need a 1-litre loaf tin, a digital scale, a large bowl and a spatula. A probe thermometer helps you check that the loaf is done.

## Method

### Day one: build the levain

Mix the levain ingredients in the evening and cover the bowl. Leave it at room temperature for 12 to 14 hours. It is ready when it has doubled and smells sour.

### Day two: mix and proof

1. Combine all final dough ingredients and stir for five minutes. Rye dough is sticky; do not knead it.
2. Rest the dough for 30 minutes.
3. Scrape it into a greased tin and smooth the top with a wet spatula.
4. Proof for 2 to 3 hours, until the surface shows small cracks.

### Day two: bake

Preheat the oven to 250 °C. Bake for 15 minutes, then lower the temperature to 200 °C and bake for another 55 minutes. The inside of the loaf should reach 98 °C.

## Cooling and storage

Let the loaf cool for at least 24 hours before slicing; the crumb is still setting. Wrapped in a linen towel, it keeps for about a week.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem              | Likely cause                  | Fix                               |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Gummy crumb          | Sliced too early              | Wait the full 24 hours            |
| Large crack on top   | Underproofed                  | Proof until small cracks appear   |
| Very sour taste      | Levain left too long          | Shorten the levain to 12 hours    |
| Flat loaf            | Weak starter                  | Feed the starter twice before use |
